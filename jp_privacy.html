Privacy Policy for "Jumping Puzzle"

Effective Date: September 2, 2026

crocodile.idle ("we," "our," or "us") operates the mobile game "Jumping Puzzle", built with Unity. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you play our game on Android and iOS.

Personal Information & Accounts. "Jumping Puzzle" does not require user accounts, registration, or logins. We do not directly ask for, collect, or store personal identifiers such as your full name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

Third-Party Data Collection & Integrated SDKs. While we do not collect personal data directly, "Jumping Puzzle" uses integrated third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) to support gameplay features, analytics, monetization, and in-app purchases. These services may collect technical device data automatically:

Unity Ads & Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies). Collects device identifiers (Android Advertising ID / GAID), IP address, device specifications, ad interaction metrics, game performance, and gameplay telemetry.

Google Firebase (Google LLC). Collects installation IDs, system performance metrics, crash logs (Firebase Crashlytics), and anonymous usage analytics to help us diagnose bugs and optimize stability.

In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing / Unity IAP). Processes transaction history, purchase tokens, and item identifiers when you buy in-app items. Note: We do not collect or store credit card details or bank information; all payments are processed securely by the Google Play Store.

How We Use Collected Information. Data processed by third-party services is used to:

Deliver core gameplay functionality and store virtual purchase entitlements.

Analyze game performance, crash reports, and player progression to fix bugs and improve content.

Display contextual and personalized advertisements.

Maintain network security and prevent billing or ad fraud.

Children's Privacy. "Jumping Puzzle" is intended for a general audience of all ages. We do not knowingly target children under 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions) with personalized advertising or unauthorized data collection. Integrated third-party SDKs are configured to comply with applicable data protection regulations for general audiences.

Third-Party Privacy Policies. To learn more about how our service providers handle your data, please review their privacy policies:

Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Google & Firebase Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your Data Rights & Choices.

Advertising Opt-Out: You can limit or reset interest-based advertising through your Android settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Purchase Management: All purchases are managed through your Google Account.

Data Clearing: Uninstalling "Jumping Puzzle" removes all locally saved game files from your device.
