Privacy Policy

Introvert Island · by crocodile.idle
Effective date: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the game Introvert Island (the "Game"), developed by crocodile.idle ("we", "us"), handles information when you play it on Android, iOS, desktop or the web.

In short: we do not ask for your name, email or any account, and we do not run our own servers that collect your data. Your game progress stays on your device. The mobile versions show ads through a third-party ad service, which receives some technical device data to deliver those ads.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following data locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data is never sent to us.

DataPurpose
Game progress (current day, level, experience, pearls)Saving your progress
Selected talentsRemembering your upgrades
Settings (music, sound effects, evening mode)Remembering your preferences

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the Game.

2. Advertising (Android and iOS only)

The mobile versions of the Game display interstitial ads using Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation by Unity Technologies, formerly ironSource) together with Unity Ads. Desktop and web versions do not show ads.

To serve ads, these services may automatically collect information such as:

  • Device information (model, operating system and version, language, screen size)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate an approximate (country/city level) location
  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID or the iOS identifier for vendors
  • App information and ad interaction data (ads shown, clicks, errors)

This information is used to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud.

Non-personalized ads. The Game tells the ad services that you have not consented to personalized (interest-based) advertising, and that your personal information must not be sold or shared ("Do Not Sell" under the CCPA). As a result, the ads you see are contextual rather than based on your profile. The Game does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites on iOS.

The ad services act under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (Unity Ads and Unity LevelPlay)
  • Google Privacy Policy (Google Play services / Advertising ID on Android)

3. Game engine

The Game is built with the Unity engine. Unity may collect limited, non-identifying technical data (such as device type, operating system and app performance information) to operate and improve its engine. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

4. What we do not collect

  • We do not require registration or ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, photos or precise location.
  • We do not offer in-app purchases or collect payment information.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

5. Your choices

  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • iOS: you can manage tracking permissions in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can play without a network connection; ads are not loaded while offline.
  • Uninstalling the Game removes all locally stored game data.

6. Children's privacy

The Game is not specifically directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the Game are served as non-personalized. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help to have it removed.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Since we do not hold any of your personal data ourselves, requests about data collected by the ad services are best directed to them using the links above. You are also welcome to contact us and we will assist where we can.

8. Data retention and transfers

Data saved on your device remains there until you clear it or uninstall the Game. Data collected by third-party services is retained and may be transferred internationally according to their own privacy policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the Game adds new features or services. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us via email.
